Church Attire

Dress Code

We would love for everyone to come in neat church attire with a graceful, celebratory feel. Soft neutrals, warm beige, champagne, cream, and gentle earth tones are warmly encouraged, while very casual wear and loud prints are best avoided.

Ivory Cream Light, soft, and classic for church.
Champagne A gentle golden neutral with a dressed-up feel.
Warm Beige Refined and easy to coordinate for families and guests.
Soft Taupe A calm earth tone that still feels polished.

For ladies

Elegant and modest

Midi dresses, soft coordinates, and graceful silhouettes in the suggested palette are warmly encouraged.

For gentlemen

Neat and refined

Button-downs, polos, dress slacks, and smart casual pieces in light neutrals or muted earth shades are ideal.
